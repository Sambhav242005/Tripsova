Phase 4 · Multi-leg journeys · one trip, many transports
A real international trip isn’t one mode. You pass a list of legs; each leg is planned with its own transport’s rules, and the clock chains (each leg departs when the previous arrives).

🗾 Example: Mumbai → Tokyo → Kyoto → Nara

✈️

Leg 1 · Flight — Mumbai → Tokyo

Single continuous segment. No fuel, no overnight road stop. Arrival time becomes Leg 2’s departure.

🚆

Leg 2 · Train — Tokyo → Kyoto

Land, but scheduled: one clean segment. Still no fuel, no roadside sleep — you’re a passenger.

🚗

Leg 3 · Car — Kyoto → Nara

Only this leg runs the driving-day logic: ⛽ fuel stops and 🌙 overnight choices (book hotel / stay with relatives / keep going) apply here — and nowhere else in the trip.

🏁 One timeline · ✈️ 🚆 🚗 stitched together

Each leg tagged with its transport · fuel & overnight only on the self-driven leg
