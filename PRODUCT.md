# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Tripova serves travellers planning trips in India first, with the product shaped to scale globally. Core users include solo travellers, families, food-restricted travellers, people seeking verified companions, and local partners who need trustworthy listing and booking workflows.

## Product Purpose

Tripova helps users plan safer, smarter, and more trustworthy trips using live traveller-verified destination data, AI trip planning, TripPod companion matching, TrustScore, PureFind food discovery, offline trip packs, and booking-ready local partner listings.

Success means a traveller can find current destination signals, create or join a relevant TripPod, plan a route, discover food that fits their diet, save useful offline information, and understand why each recommendation can be trusted.

## Brand Personality

Premium, trustworthy, minimal, useful, traveller-powered, India-first, and globally scalable. The product should feel calm, capable, current, and grounded in real traveller proof.

## Anti-references

Tripova should not feel like a generic travel app, an Instagram-style content feed, a fake demo-data prototype, a rating-only recommendation list, a SaaS gradient template, or an over-decorated interface where visual novelty competes with planning tasks.

## Design Principles

1. Trust before delight. Show verification, data freshness, source, confidence, and clear fallbacks.
2. Workflow over spectacle. Trip creation, route planning, food discovery, and offline saving should be quick and predictable.
3. Traveller-powered, not generic CRUD. Community verification and lived travel context should be visible in the product surface.
4. India-first inclusion. Dietary needs, safety, low-network use, and companion trust are core needs, not edge cases.
5. Honest states. Empty, loading, failure, and unknown-destination states should explain what happened and ask for the right next detail.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Default target: WCAG 2.2 AA for product UI. The product should support keyboard navigation, readable contrast, screen-reader labels for forms and controls, reduced-motion preferences, and inclusive flows for travellers with dietary, safety, and low-network constraints.
